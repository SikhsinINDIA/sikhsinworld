GURUDWARAS IN WEST MIDLANDS

United Kingdom
Faith  ·  Seva  ·  Sangat  ·  Heritage

Birmingham, Smethwick, West Bromwich, Wolverhampton, Walsall and Leamington have some of the largest Sikh communities in Britain, and Gurdwaras on almost every high street.

Handsworth, Birmingham

Guru Nanak Nishkam Gurdwara
Handsworth, Birmingham
Location: 18-20 Soho Road, Handsworth, Birmingham, West Midlands B21 9BH

Guru Nanak Nishkam Gurdwara, known to all as Soho Road Gurdwara, was built in the late 1970s under the leadership of Baba Puran Singh and was then the largest Gurdwara outside the subcontinent.

Visit Page
Baba Deep Singh Gurdwara
Handsworth, Birmingham
Location: 6 Oakland Road, Handsworth, Birmingham, West Midlands B21 0NA

Baba Deep Singh Gurdwara, officially Yaadgar Baba Deep Singh, stands at the other end of Soho Road from the Nishkam Gurdwara.

Visit Page

Smethwick

Guru Nanak Gurdwara Smethwick
Smethwick
Location: 128-130 High Street, Smethwick, West Midlands B66 3AP

Guru Nanak Gurdwara Smethwick opened in 1958 in a former church on the High Street and is one of the oldest and largest Gurdwaras in the Midlands.

Visit Page
Gurdwara Akaal Bunga Sahib
Smethwick
Location: Smethwick, West Midlands B67 6BJ

Gurdwara Akaal Bunga Sahib, in the streets behind Smethwick High Street, is a former church that still looks like one, with a tall fortress-like tower crowned by the Nishan Sahib.

Visit Page

Wednesfield

Guru Nanak Gurdwara Wednesfield
Wednesfield
Location: Well Lane, Wednesfield, West Midlands WV11 1XT

Guru Nanak Gurdwara Wednesfield is one of the large Gurdwaras built in the Black Country around the turn of the millennium, with several darbar halls and classes in Punjabi, Gurmat and gatka.

Visit Page

Wolverhampton

Nanaksar Thath Isher Darbar Wolverhampton
Wolverhampton
Location: Mander Street, Wolverhampton, West Midlands WV3 0JU

Nanaksar Thath Isher Darbar, near Wolverhampton city centre, is one of the city’s three great Gurdwaras.

Visit Page
Guru Nanak Gurdwara Sedgley Street
Wolverhampton
Location: Sedgley Street, Blakenhall, Wolverhampton, West Midlands WV2 3AJ

Guru Nanak Gurdwara on Sedgley Street is the heart of Sikh life in Wolverhampton, a city where Sikhs are the second largest faith and, as the saying goes, every Sikh has a relative.

Visit Page

Walsall

Guru Nanak Gurdwara Caldmore
Walsall
Location: 127 West Bromwich Street, Caldmore, Walsall, West Midlands WS1 4DE

Guru Nanak Gurdwara in Caldmore began in 1962, when local Sikhs rented the British Lion Club as a Gurdwara.

Visit Page

West Bromwich

Guru Har Rai Gurdwara
West Bromwich
Location: 126-128 High Street, West Bromwich, West Midlands B70 6JW

Guru Har Rai Gurdwara was founded in 1979 by Baba Harbans Singh Domeli Wale and was among the first in Britain to hold evening diwans on weekdays.

Visit Page

Warwick

Gurdwara Sahib Leamington and Warwick
Warwick
Location: Tachbrook Park Drive, Warwick, West Midlands CV34 6RH

Gurdwara Sahib Leamington and Warwick is one of the largest Gurdwaras in Britain.
