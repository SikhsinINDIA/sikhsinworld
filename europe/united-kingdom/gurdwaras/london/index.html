GURUDWARAS IN LONDON

United Kingdom
Faith  ·  Seva  ·  Sangat  ·  Heritage

London’s Sikhs first settled near Heathrow in Southall and Hounslow, and today large sangats live across west and east London, from Southall to Ilford.

Southall

Gurdwara Sri Guru Singh Sabha Southall
Southall
Location: Guru Nanak Road (formerly Havelock Road), Southall, London UB2 4NP

Sri Guru Singh Sabha, known to all as Southall Gurdwara, is the largest Gurdwara outside India.
