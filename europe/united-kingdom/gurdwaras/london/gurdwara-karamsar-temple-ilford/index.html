Gurdwara Karamsar Temple is a Gurdwara in Ilford, London, United Kingdom. It dates from 2005. Like every Gurdwara, its doors are open to everyone, whatever their faith, to sit in the sangat, listen to Gurbani and share langar. Diwan and langar times vary, so it is worth checking locally before you visit.
