Nanaksar Gurdwara is a Gurdwara in Toronto, Ontario, Canada. It follows the Nanaksar tradition, known for its round-the-clock kirtan and simple langar. Like every Gurdwara, its doors are open to everyone, whatever their faith, to sit in the sangat, listen to Gurbani and share langar. Diwan and langar times vary, so it is worth checking locally before you visit.
