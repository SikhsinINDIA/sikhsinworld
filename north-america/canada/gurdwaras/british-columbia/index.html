GURUDWARAS IN BRITISH COLUMBIA

Canada
Faith  ·  Seva  ·  Sangat  ·  Heritage

Canadian Sikh history began in British Columbia. Sikhs arrived in the early 1900s and opened the country’s first Gurdwara in Vancouver in 1908. Today the biggest sangats are in Surrey, Abbotsford and the rest of the Lower Mainland, with Gurdwaras as far north as Fort St. John and Terrace.

Abbotsford

Gur Sikh Temple
Abbotsford
Location: 33089 S Fraser Way, Abbotsford, BC

The Gur Sikh Temple in Abbotsford is tied to one of the most important places in Canadian Sikh history: the temple built by Sikh mill workers on South Fraser Way and completed in 1911, the oldest surviving Gurdwara in North America and a National Historic Site of Canada.
