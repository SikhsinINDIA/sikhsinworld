Sikh Association of Australia, Penrith
Jamisontown (Penrith)
15-27 Blaikie Road, Jamisontown, NSW 2750  ·  Australia

The Sikh Association of Australia runs the Gurdwara on Blaikie Road in Jamisontown, Penrith, at the foot of the Blue Mountains. It serves Sikh families across Penrith and the outer west of Sydney with daily prayers, kirtan and langar.
