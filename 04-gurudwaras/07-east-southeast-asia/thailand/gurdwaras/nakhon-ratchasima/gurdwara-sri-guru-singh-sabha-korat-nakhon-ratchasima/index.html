Gurdwara Sri Guru Singh Sabha Korat
Nakhon Ratchasima (Korat)
3 Soi Sahaphan, Chomphon Road, Nakhon Ratchasima, Nakhon Ratchasima 30000  ·  Thailand

Sikhs began settling in Korat for business in 1947 and held prayers in their homes until the sangat was large enough to build a Gurdwara. It opened on 23 December 1984 with a parade through the town. The two-storey building has a golden gate and a small garden in front, the langar hall downstairs and the darbar hall above.
