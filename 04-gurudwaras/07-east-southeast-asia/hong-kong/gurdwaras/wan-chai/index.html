GURUDWARAS IN WAN CHAI

Hong Kong
Faith  ·  Seva  ·  Sangat  ·  Heritage

Hong Kong’s one Gurdwara stands on Queen’s Road East in Wan Chai, on Hong Kong Island, on land given to the Sikh community more than a century ago.

Khalsa Diwan Sikh Temple
Wan Chai
Location: 371 Queen’s Road East, Wan Chai

The first Gurdwara in Hong Kong, Sri Guru Singh Sabha, was built in 1901 by Sikh soldiers of a British Army regiment, on land given by the government.
