GURUDWARAS IN NEW SOUTH WALES

Australia
Faith  ·  Seva  ·  Sangat  ·  Heritage

New South Wales has Australia’s oldest Gurdwaras, on the north coast at Woolgoolga, and its largest Sikh community in western and south-western Sydney. There are also sangats in Newcastle, Griffith and Murwillumbah.

Glenwood

Gurdwara Sahib Glenwood
Glenwood
Location: 10 Meurants Lane, Glenwood, NSW 2768

The Gurdwara Sahib at Glenwood, in Sydney’s north-west, is run by the Australian Sikh Association, which describes itself as the largest registered Sikh body in the Southern Hemisphere.

Revesby

Sri Guru Singh Sabha
Revesby
Location: 14-18 The River Road, Revesby, NSW 2212

Sri Guru Singh Sabha in Revesby, in south-western Sydney, opened in 1978 in two houses bought from the Sydney City Mission.

Visit Page

Austral

Sikh Mission Centre
Austral
Location: 170 Ninth Avenue, Austral, NSW 2171

The Sikh Mission Centre in Austral serves the fast-growing suburbs of south-western Sydney.

Visit Page

Minto

Simran House
Minto
Location: 23 Lincoln Street, Minto, NSW 2566

Simran House on Lincoln Street in Minto serves the Sikh families of Campbelltown and Sydney’s outer south-west.

Visit Page

Wentworthville

Ramgarhia Welfare Association of Australia
Wentworthville
Location: Banksia Room, Wentworthville Community Centre, 2 Lane Street, Wentworthville, NSW 2145

The Ramgarhia Welfare Association of Australia holds its diwan in the Banksia Room of the Wentworthville Community Centre, near Parramatta.

Visit Page

Jamisontown

Sikh Association of Australia, Penrith
Jamisontown (Penrith)
Location: 15-27 Blaikie Road, Jamisontown, NSW 2750

The Sikh Association of Australia runs the Gurdwara on Blaikie Road in Jamisontown, Penrith, at the foot of the Blue Mountains.

Visit Page

Turramurra

Turramurra Gurdwara
Turramurra
Location: 81 Kissing Point Road, Turramurra, NSW 2074

The Gurdwara on Kissing Point Road in Turramurra, also known as Guru Nanak Gurdwara, has served Sydney’s upper north shore since around 1979, making it one of the oldest Gurdwaras in Sydney.

Visit Page

Shortland

Newcastle Sikh Association
Shortland (Newcastle)
Location: 27 Marton Street, Shortland, NSW

The Newcastle Sikh Association runs the Gurdwara on Marton Street in Shortland, serving Sikh families and University of Newcastle students in the Hunter region with prayer, kirtan and langar.

Visit Page

Yoogali

Sikh Association of Griffith
Yoogali (Griffith)
Location: 13 Edon Street, Yoogali, NSW 2680

The Sikh Association of Griffith runs a Gurdwara in Yoogali, on the edge of Griffith in the Riverina.

Visit Page

Woolgoolga

Guru Nanak Sikh Temple
Woolgoolga
Location: 13 River Street, Woolgoolga, NSW 2456

Guru Nanak Sikh Temple in Woolgoolga, on the New South Wales north coast, was the town’s second Gurdwara, built at the end of the 1960s.

Visit Page
The First Sikh Temple
Woolgoolga
Location: 14 Hastings Street, Woolgoolga, NSW 2456

The First Sikh Temple in Woolgoolga was officially opened on 8 June 1968 and is believed to be Australia’s first Gurdwara.

Visit Page

Murwillumbah

Murwillumbah Sikh Temple
Murwillumbah
Location: 29 Nullum Street, Murwillumbah, NSW 2484

Murwillumbah Sikh Temple serves the Sikh families of the Tweed Valley in far northern New South Wales, near the Queensland border.
