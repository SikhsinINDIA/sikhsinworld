GURUDWARAS IN MANITOBA

Canada
Faith  ·  Seva  ·  Sangat  ·  Heritage

Almost all of Manitoba’s Gurdwaras are in and around Winnipeg, where the sangat has grown quickly over the last twenty years.

Winnipeg

Guru Nanak Darbar
Winnipeg
Location: 900 McLeod Ave, Winnipeg, MB R2G 2T7

Guru Nanak Darbar has served Winnipeg’s sangat since 1994 from a 14,400 square foot building on McLeod Avenue.

Gurdwara Nanaksar
Winnipeg
Location: 255 St David Rd, Winnipeg, MB

Gurdwara Nanaksar in Winnipeg began in 1982, when the local sangat bought a church on St David Road, borrowing against their own homes, after a visit by Sant Baba Gurdev Singh Ji of Nanaksar.
