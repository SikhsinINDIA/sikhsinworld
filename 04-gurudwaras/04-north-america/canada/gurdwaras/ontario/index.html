GURUDWARAS IN ONTARIO

Canada
Faith  ·  Seva  ·  Sangat  ·  Heritage

Ontario is home to Canada’s largest Sikh population. Brampton, Mississauga and the rest of Peel Region have the most Gurdwaras, and sangats stretch from Windsor and London in the south-west to Ottawa and Cornwall in the east.
