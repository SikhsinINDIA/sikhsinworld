GURUDWARAS IN PUNJAB

Pakistan
Faith  ·  Seva  ·  Sangat  ·  Heritage

Gurdwaras in Bhoman Shah, Eminabad, Hasan Abdal, Kartarpur, Narowal, Lahore and more.

Bhoman Shah

Gurdwara Baba Bhuman Shah
Bhoman Shah
Location: Bhoman Shah, Punjab

Gurdwara Baba Bhuman Shah, near Dipalpur in Okara district, honours Baba Bhuman Shah, an 18th-century Udasi saint known for his langar and devotion to Guru Nanak Dev Ji.

Visit Page

Eminabad

Gurdwara Rori Sahib
Eminabad
Location: Eminabad, Punjab 52370

Gurdwara Rori Sahib at Eminabad, near Gujranwala, marks a place where Guru Nanak Dev Ji stayed, sitting on a bed of pebbles (rori) during his travels.

Visit Page

Hasan Abdal

Photo: Muhammad Imran Saeed, CC BY 2.0, via Wikimedia Commons
Panja Sahib
Hasan Abdal
Location: Hasan Abdal, Punjab 43730

Gurdwara Panja Sahib at Hasan Abdal holds the rock that, in Sikh tradition, bears the imprint of Guru Nanak Dev Ji’s hand, where he stopped a boulder rolled down the hill by Wali Qandhari.

Visit Page

Kartarpur, Narowal

Photo: Xubayr Mayo, CC BY-SA 3.0, via Wikimedia Commons
Gurdwara Darbar Sahib Kartarpur
Kartarpur, Narowal
Location: Kartarpur, Narowal, Punjab

Gurdwara Darbar Sahib at Kartarpur, on the Ravi river, stands where Guru Nanak Dev Ji spent the last eighteen years of his life, farming the land and teaching, and where he passed away in 1539.

Visit Page

Lahore

Gurdwara Dera Sahib
Lahore
Location: Lahore, Punjab

Gurdwara Dera Sahib in Lahore, beside the Badshahi Mosque and the Lahore Fort, marks the place of the martyrdom of Guru Arjan Dev Ji in 1606.

Visit Page
Gurdwara Janam Asthan Guru Ram Das
Lahore
Location: Lahore, Punjab

Gurdwara Janam Asthan Guru Ram Das, in the Chuna Mandi bazaar of old Lahore, marks the birthplace of Guru Ram Das Ji, the fourth Guru, in 1534.

Visit Page
Old Gurudwara Chhevin Patshahi
Lahore
Location: Lahore, Punjab 54100

Gurdwara Chhevin Patshahi in Lahore is linked to Guru Hargobind Sahib Ji, the sixth Guru, who came to Lahore to see where his father, Guru Arjan Dev Ji, had been martyred.

Visit Page
Shaheed Ganj Gurdwara
Lahore
Location: Lahore, Punjab

Gurdwara Shaheed Ganj, near Landa Bazaar in Lahore, honours the Sikhs who were martyred here in the 18th century, including Bhai Mani Singh Ji and Bhai Taru Singh Ji.

Visit Page

Manhala

Gurudwara Baba Baloo Hasna
Manhala
Location: Baba Khairra St, Manhala, Punjab

Gurudwara Baba Baloo Hasna is a historic Sikh shrine in Manhala, Punjab, Pakistan.

Mattoo Bhaike

Gurudwara Khara Sahib
Mattoo Bhaike
Location: Mattoo Bhaike, Punjab

Gurudwara Khara Sahib is a historic Sikh shrine in Mattoo Bhaike, Punjab, Pakistan.

Nankana Sahib

Photo: Jeev at English Wikipedia, Public domain, via Wikimedia Commons
Gurdwara Janam Asthan
Nankana Sahib
Location: Nankana Sahib, Punjab 39100

Gurdwara Janam Asthan in Nankana Sahib marks the place where Guru Nanak Dev Ji was born in 1469, in the village then called Rai Bhoi di Talwandi.

Visit Page

Narwar

Gurudwara Sandhawalia
Narwar
Location: Narwar, Punjab

Gurudwara Sandhawalia is a historic Sikh shrine in Narwar, Punjab, Pakistan.

Visit Page

Rohtas

Gurdwara Chowa Sahib
Rohtas
Location: Rohtas, Punjab

Gurdwara Chowa Sahib, inside the great Rohtas Fort near Jhelum, marks the spring that, in Sikh tradition, appeared when Guru Nanak Dev Ji touched the ground here during his travels.

Visit Page

Sialkot

Photo: Sarmad Mahmud, CC BY-SA 3.0, via Wikimedia Commons
Gurdwara Beri Sahib
Sialkot
Location: Sialkot, Punjab 51310

Gurdwara Beri Sahib in Sialkot stands beside the ber tree under which, in Sikh tradition, Guru Nanak Dev Ji rested when he visited the city.
